The short version

Burgers in a bar, fries on the house.

Burger Boys lives inside Saving Grace, a laneway bar on Church Lane in the Melbourne CBD. Order at the counter, grab a stool, and we'll bring the basket over — patties smashed to order, chicken twice-fried for the crunch, and a side of fries that's never on the bill.

Everything's made in-house: the pickles, the bourbon BBQ, the spiced garlic mayo, Grace's own secret sauce. Beef is Black Angus, chicken is buttermilk-brined, and the vegan and mushroom builds get the same treatment as everything else on the pass.
